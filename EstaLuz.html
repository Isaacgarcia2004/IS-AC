Esta Luz Nunca Se Apagara

The piece, “Esta Luz Nunca Se Apagará,” is a social commentary piece, made with the intention of destigmatizing the way that media portrays undocumented people and immigrants. People who are undocumented, or “illegal aliens,” as they’re often called by big media, are often shown to be aggressive, thieving criminals. This, however, is far from the truth. Undocumented people are fathers, mothers, children, everyday people who work hard for their family, the things they love, and overall, to live a better life. This piece shows the real lives of immigrants and immigrant families. This piece contains snippets of 8mm tapes from my childhood. Being a child of immigrants, gives me perspective and the ability to know the truth and ignore the ignorant stereotypes produced by the media. (Photo of end title)
